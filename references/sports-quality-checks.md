# Sports quality checks

Probe and decode the full export to check stream presence, orientation, dimensions, frame rate, duration, timestamps, color metadata and corruption. Compare output duration to the timeline, including speed ramps and overlaps. Verify audio presence and final loudness/peaks against the chosen specification.

Play every selected highlight and every speed/effect transition when possible. Check that setup and outcome remain comprehensible, the athlete and ball stay in frame, and tracking does not jitter or obscure play. Review slow motion frame by frame at contact, release, crossing and landing for duplicated frames or interpolation errors. Check replay labeling and that score/clock graphics are consistent with the actual event.

Verify all names, jerseys, stats and claims against provided or verified sources. Ensure overlays, source credit and captions remain readable and clear of decisive action. Inspect landscape and portrait versions separately; passing one crop does not verify the other.

Listen to the actual output for clicks, clipping, musical timing, commentary clarity and fake-sounding effects. Images and waveforms do not prove audible quality. If playback is unavailable, label that check incomplete. Record reviewed ranges and defects in project QC notes; do not claim full review from spot checks.

Preserve sources, the output-to-source timeline and asset provenance. Do not publish automatically. Existing exports stay available when a revision is made.
